# Security

This policy covers every Lobstack-ai repository that does not have its own:
lobstack.ai, the Lobstack API (Gateway), the Console, the Lobstack app, and the
public SDK, CLI and MCP repositories.

## Report a vulnerability

Report it privately on GitHub:
**[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/Lobstack-ai/.github/security/advisories/new)**

Only the Lobstack team can see the report. Please do not open a public issue,
pull request or discussion about it. If the problem is in one of the public
repositories (lobstack-gateway, lobstack-cli, lobstack-mcp), you can also use
the **Report a vulnerability** button on that repository's Security tab.

Include what you found, where (URL, endpoint, app version, operating system),
how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it.

## What to expect

We are a small team. We aim to reply within three business days and to assess
the report within ten. We will tell you what we decide and when a fix ships.

Please keep the details private until a fix ships or 90 days have passed since
your report, whichever comes first. We will agree the date with you.

There is no bug bounty. With your permission, we credit you in the published
advisory.

## Good-faith research

Good-faith research is welcome. Test only your own accounts, keep no one
else's data, and do not degrade the service, and we will not take legal action
over it.

Out of scope: denial of service, spam, social engineering, and attacks that
need your computer to be compromised already.
